1 | Naomi | Narration | Thunder rumbled off in the distance, yet there was no sign of a storm. Clear blue skies and a light breeze, the only thing out of place was a massive pit surrounded by geometric markings on the ground. It reminded me of what we flew out of in the desert. We had to be miles and miles away to see it so completely, even this high up. Grazing animals were like ants as they meandered between their several groups around the gaping abyss. Oblivious to the rising ground I impacted and deflected down the sloped hills. Skimming the tops of the green and brown blades, I braced for the short fall as I disengaged the Decoherence Mechanism.
